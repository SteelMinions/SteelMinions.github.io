About Us

Steel Minions is a commercial outreach initiative for the Computer Science for Games course. It is a registered company which can be used for industrial and research game development projects with the involvement of staff and students. Activities involve: Placement Supervision Project supervision Complying with accreditation regulations and meeting HSE goals Commercial publishing of student projects on PC, Web, Android, iOS, PlayStation5, PlayStation4 and PlayStation4-VR Liaising with industrial clients and students Liaising with research staff and students Facilitating and mentoring student involvement in national competitions.
